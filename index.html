# Privacy Policy for GamePointsCounterApp **Effective date: August 1, 2026** GamePointsCounterApp (the **"App"**) is a free scorekeeping application supported by advertising. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App. ## 1. Developer and contact The App is developed and published by PIAFTECH (POLAND). For privacy questions or requests, contact: **piaftech.mobile@gmail.com** ## 2. Information handled by the App The App does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, telephone number, contacts, precise location, photographs, or payment information. Scores and timer information entered in the App are used only to provide the scorekeeping functionality. The current version of the App does not transmit this information to the developer or to an external server. Temporary state may be kept on the device so that scores and timer information can survive a screen rotation or another recreation of the App's screen. ## 3. Advertising and information handled by Google The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertisements and the Google User Messaging Platform SDK (UMP) to manage consent choices where required. According to Google's documentation, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share information for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes, including: - the device's IP address, which may be used to estimate general location; - interactions with the App and advertisements, such as app launches, taps, and video views; - diagnostic and performance information, such as app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage; and - device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID, App Set ID, and other identifiers where applicable. Information transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. Google may use this information to provide, personalize, measure, and protect advertising, subject to the user's consent choices and Google's policies. For more information, see: - [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) - [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) - [Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure) - [Google advertising controls](https://myadcenter.google.com/) ## 4. Consent and advertising choices Where required by applicable law, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent before requesting advertisements that require consent. Depending on your location and the configuration of the advertising service, you may be able to consent to personalized advertising, refuse consent, or manage other privacy choices. You can also manage or delete your Android advertising ID in your device settings and manage Google advertising preferences through My Ad Center. The legal basis for processing that requires consent is your consent. Certain limited processing may also be necessary for security, fraud prevention, compliance with legal obligations, and delivery of non-personalized or limited advertisements, as permitted by applicable law. ## 5. Information received by the developer The developer may receive aggregated advertising and performance reports from AdMob. These reports are intended to provide statistics such as advertisement impressions, clicks, and estimated revenue. The developer does not use the App to obtain the contents of your scores or timer directly from your device. If you contact the developer by email, the developer will receive your email address and any information you voluntarily include in your message. This information will be used to respond to your request and may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the correspondence, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes. ## 6. Data sharing The App does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Google and its service providers in connection with advertising, consent management, analytics, security, and fraud prevention. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the developer, or third parties. ## 7. Data retention The developer does not operate a server that stores scores or timer information from the App. Google determines the retention periods for information processed through AdMob and related services in accordance with Google's policies and the applicable advertising configuration. Email correspondence is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy. ## 8. International data transfers Google and its service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, such transfers are subject to safeguards provided under applicable data-protection law. More information is available in Google's Privacy Policy. ## 9. Children's privacy The App is a general-purpose scorekeeping tool and is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through an account or registration form. Parents or guardians who believe that a child has provided personal information by contacting the developer may request its deletion at **piaftech.mobile@gmail.com**. The developer must configure the App's target audience and advertising settings in Google Play Console and AdMob consistently with the audience to which the App is actually offered. ## 10. Your privacy rights Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the right to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or withdrawal of consent. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. To make a request concerning information controlled by the developer, contact **piaftech.mobile@gmail.com**. For information controlled by Google, use Google's privacy tools or contact Google as explained in its Privacy Policy. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before consent was withdrawn. ## 11.
